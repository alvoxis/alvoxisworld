    Controls

    • One finger / left mouse: use the selected tool. Hold to keep applying.
    • Two fingers: pan and pinch-zoom. Mouse: right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom.

    • ↶ Undo: reverts the last terrain change (brushes, painting, meteor and earthquake terrain). Ctrl/Cmd+Z on a keyboard.
    • 🗺 Minimap: tap or drag on it to move the camera.
